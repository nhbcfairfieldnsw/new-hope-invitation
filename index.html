Welcome to New Hope

“Come to Me, all who are weary and heavy-laden, and I will give you rest.”
Matthew 11:28

1. Character

A person who is searching for truth, hope that lasts and a place to belong. They may be new to the faith or returning after years away.

2. Problem

Life feels uncertain, and church feels like a room of strangers. Underneath that is the deeper problem: sin separates us from God, and no effort of ours closes the gap.

3. Guide

Jesus Christ, the only Saviour. New Hope Baptist Church is a small congregation that was once strangers too, and we hold to Scripture and the 1689 Confession.

4. Plan

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Come on a Sunday at 11am.

  2. 2

    Hear God’s Word read and preached.

  3. 3

    Stay afterwards and ask your questions.

5. Call to action

Come this Sunday at 11am to the Brian Wunsch Centre.

6. Failure avoided

Going on without knowing the Saviour leaves the soul in danger.

7. Success

Knowing Christ, being made new in him and belonging to his people.

Who we are

Biblical

Grounded in the authority and sufficiency of God’s Word.

Christ centred

Exalting Jesus Christ in preaching, worship and life.

Worshipping

Gathering to glorify God in spirit and truth.

Expository

Committed to faithfully proclaiming the meaning of Scripture.

Growing

Encouraging spiritual maturity through God’s Word.

Prayerful

Depending upon God through earnest prayer.

Devoted

Committed to following Christ and obeying His Word.

Fellowshipping

Sharing life together as God’s people.

Evangelistic

Proclaiming the gospel and calling people to Christ.

Grace filled

Reflecting God’s grace in our relationships and ministry.

Disciple making

Committed to teaching believers to follow Christ, obey His commands, and grow in spiritual maturity.

Mission minded

Seeking to make Christ known through the proclamation of the gospel and faithful Christian living.
